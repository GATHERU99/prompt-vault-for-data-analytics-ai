Client Discovery Framework

Business

- What does your business do?
- Who are your customers?
- What are your main revenue streams?

Marketing

- How do customers currently find you?
- What marketing channels are working?

Customer Experience

- What questions do customers ask repeatedly?
- What causes customers to hesitate?

Digital Presence

- Website?
- Social media?
- Virtual tour?

Goals

- More bookings?
- More foot traffic?
- Better engagement?
- Better lead quality?

Success Metric

What would make this project a success?
