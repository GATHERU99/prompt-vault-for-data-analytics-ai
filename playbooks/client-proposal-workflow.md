Client Proposal Workflow

Goal

Create proposals that convert prospects into paying clients.

---

Step 1: Discovery

Understand:

- Client business
- Client goals
- Client pain points
- Current process
- Desired outcome

---

Step 2: Define the Problem

Write:

What problem is costing the client money, time, or opportunities?

---

Step 3: Define the Solution

Describe:

- Service
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Expected outcomes

---

Step 4: Quantify Value

Estimate:

- Revenue increase
- Time saved
- Efficiency gains
- Customer experience improvements

---

Step 5: Handle Objections

Address:

- Cost
- Risk
- Timeline
- Complexity

---

Step 6: Pricing

Include:

- Package
- Cost
- Payment terms

---

Step 7: Call To Action

State clearly:

- Next meeting
- Approval process
- Project start date

---

Proposal Checklist

- Clear problem
- Clear solution
- Clear value
- Clear pricing
- Clear next step

If any are missing, revise.
