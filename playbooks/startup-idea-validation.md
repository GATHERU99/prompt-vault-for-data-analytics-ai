Startup Idea Validation

Goal

Determine whether a startup idea deserves time, money, and effort.

---

Step 1: Problem Check

Ask:

- What problem does this solve?
- Who has this problem?
- How painful is it?

Score: /10

---

Step 2: Market Check

Ask:

- How many people have this problem?
- Are they paying for solutions already?
- Is demand growing?

Score: /10

---

Step 3: Solution Check

Ask:

- Is the solution simple?
- Is it differentiated?
- Is it easier than alternatives?

Score: /10

---

Step 4: Competition Check

Identify:

- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Market leaders

Ask:

Why would someone choose us?

Score: /10

---

Step 5: Revenue Check

Define:

- Customer
- Pricing model
- Revenue streams

Score: /10

---

Step 6: Feasibility Check

Evaluate:

- Skills required
- Time required
- Capital required

Score: /10

---

Step 7: MVP Design

Define:

- Smallest usable version
- First customers
- Success metric
