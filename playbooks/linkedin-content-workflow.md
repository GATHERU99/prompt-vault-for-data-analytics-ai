LinkedIn Content Workflow

Goal

Create LinkedIn posts that build authority, attract opportunities, and generate conversations.

---

Step 1: Capture

Collect ideas from:

- Work experiences
- Data projects
- Client interactions
- Business observations
- Industry trends
- Personal lessons

Question:

What happened that is worth sharing?

---

Step 2: Find the Insight

Ask:

- What surprised me?
- What did I learn?
- What mistake did I avoid?
- What would help someone else?

Write one key takeaway.

---

Step 3: Choose a Post Type

Select one:

- Lesson learned
- Case study
- Behind the scenes
- Industry opinion
- Data insight
- Career story
- Client success story

---

Step 4: Structure

Hook

↓

Story

↓

Insight

↓

Call to action

---

Step 5: Publish Checklist

- Clear hook
- Short paragraphs
- Valuable insight
- Professional tone
- CTA included

---

Step 6: Repurpose

Convert the post into:

- Carousel
- Instagram caption
- X post
- Blog article
- Video script

---

Success Metrics

Track:

- Impressions
- Comments
- Shares
- Profile visits
- Leads generated

---

Final Question

Does this post help my audience?

If no, rewrite it.
